Good to know

Questions, answered before you ask.

What's included in a free trial day?

A full day in the focus room with a desk and monitor, one guided recovery session, and all the coffee, tea, electrolytes and filtered air you'd get as a member — so you can feel the difference before you commit.

Where are you, and when are you open?

Tööstuse 43-100, at the Volta Tn corner in Tallinn (entrance from the Volta Tn side). Workspace access during the day; the private event space is available evenings 17:00–22:00.

Do I need to know anything about biohacking?

Not at all. The recovery tech is guided and optional — most members start with sauna and red-light and go deeper as they're curious.

Can I book the studio or event space without a membership?

Yes. Both are bookable on their own; members simply get preferred rates and priority availability.
